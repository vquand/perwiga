# Honkai: Star Rail module

- Module ID: `honkai-star-rail`.
- Scope: Honkai: Star Rail only.
- Follow [games/AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and the root data-safety rules.
- Keep Star Rail-specific entity types, relations, fields, event sources, parsers, fixtures, migrations, and custom UI in this module.
- Do not assume an API, RSS feed, region schedule, or time zone until it is documented from a confirmed source. The current catalog source is a community-maintained game-data extract, not an official HoYoverse API.
- Shared HoYoverse helpers are allowed only for behavior proven common; do not depend directly on the Genshin Impact module.
- The reviewable `data/characters.json`, `data/light-cones.json`, `data/relic-sets.json`, `data/relics.json`, `data/paths.json`, and `data/elements.json` snapshots are generated by `scripts/fetch-data.py` from four StarRailRes locales at pinned commit `d226befe3db13f2ec15f4161d5f34b1b607643fe`, checked on 2026-09-01. Preserve the source IDs, locale joins, parent Relic Set links, and explicit asset paths.
- The current StarRailRes source snapshot contains 97 Character records, 169 Light Cones, 60 Relic Sets, 742 Relic Pieces, 9 Paths, and 7 Elements. The separate HoYoWiki snapshot contains 436 NPC records joined to 433 English names; preserve its `entry_page_id` identities and keep the three English gaps source-only rather than inventing names.
- Hán-Việt data lives in separate additive snapshots: `character-han-viet.json`, `light-cone-han-viet.json`, `relic-han-viet.json`, `taxonomy-han-viet.json`, and `npc-han-viet.json`. Generated aliases are search aids, marked unofficial, and must never overwrite official English, Chinese, or Vietnamese fields. Preserve unresolved characters and source-literal names for later review.
- The crawler never opens SQLite, validates untrusted JSON, writes snapshots atomically, and refuses unexpected catalog shrinkage unless `--allow-shrink` is explicitly supplied after review. Star Rail imports are additive and source-keyed, preserving personal edits and official Traditional Chinese as a provenance-labeled alias. Source placeholders such as `{NICKNAME}` remain in official data; UI-only contextual labels may identify the Trailblazer variant.
